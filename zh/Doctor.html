# 2.3 连续体机械臂的形态估计

仪器仪表学报在审文章基于切向量拟合的连续体机械臂多IMU形态估计 摘要如下： 针对中心杆构型的连续体机械臂没有关节结构、形态反馈难以解决的问题，本文提出了一种基于切向量拟合的连续体机械臂多IMU形态估计算法。首先基于Cosserat杆理论对连续体机械臂进行数学建模，采用误差状态卡尔曼滤波获取各测量位置处的形态并求解切向量，然后通过B样条方法拟合以弧长为自变量的切向量函数。根据Cosserat杆模型对连续变化的切向量函数进行积分完成形态估计。将所提算法应用于连续体机械臂样机并进行了动态轨迹和静态负载试验。实验结果表明，与以往的基于分段常曲率假设的形态估计方法相比，所提算法末端位置的定位精度和形态重构的准确性显著提高。在形态因负载产生较大变化时，所提算法也表现出更好的鲁棒性和稳定性，误差相比现有方法降低了50%以上。

# 2.4 超冗余机械臂的形态估计

IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation & Measurement在审文章 Compensation Method for Lever-Arm Effect in Multi-IMU Measurement of Cable-Driven Hyper-Redundant Manipulators 摘要如下： Owing to structural design limitations, it is usually challenging to install traditional encoders or other joint measurement devices on cable-driven hyper-redundant manipulators (CDHRMs). As an alternative, IMUs can be used to measure the relative attitude of adjacent links to obtain joint information. However, the offset between the IMU's installation position and the rigid body's center of rotation can cause a lever-arm effect, which may increase joint angle measurement errors and degrade the accuracy of the manipulator's end-effector trajectory estimation. This paper proposes a lever-arm effect compensation method for a distributed multi-IMU system to improve the accuracy of end-effector trajectory estimation. Firstly, we establish the forward kinematics model of a hyper-redundant manipulator based on screw theory. Subsequently, the mechanism of the lever arm effect is reanalyzed using screw theory. Based on this mechanism and in conjunction with the kinematic model, a compensation method for the lever-arm effect in a distributed multi-IMU measurement system is proposed. Finally, we developed a pose estimation algorithm for the manipulator end-effector based on distributed IMUs, which remains highly effective even in the presence of the lever-arm effect. Our method is validated both by simulations and practical experiments. In the prototype experiment of the end-effector trajectory estimation task, we design and manufacture a distributed multi-IMU measurement system. The result shows that the trajectory root mean square error (RMSE) is reduced to 4.78 mm (1.83% of the manipulator's total length), demonstrating the superiority of our approach.
